Reporting a Death to Social Security and Medicare (USAGov)

USAGov says you must report a beneficiary's death to the Social Security Administration (SSA), which handles death reports for both Social Security and Medicare. Its page was last updated August 31, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

How to report

  • Give the person's Social Security number to the funeral director so they can report the death to the SSA
  • Contact your local Social Security office, or call 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778)

USAGov says the SSA only accepts death reports by phone or in person, not by email or online. If you report it yourself, you can begin without the death certificate, but you will need it later to complete the report.

Returning a payment

The SSA cannot pay benefits for the month of a recipient's death. For example, if the person died in July, the payment received in August (for July) must be returned. USAGov points to how to return a check. For direct deposit, notify the financial institution as soon as possible and ask it to return that month's payment and any that arrived later.

Survivor benefits

Spouses and minor children may be eligible for Social Security survivor benefits. USAGov points to the SSA's survivor benefits page for eligibility, monthly amounts, and how to apply.
