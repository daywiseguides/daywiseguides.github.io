What Is a Debt-to-Income Ratio and How Do You Calculate It? (CFPB)

The CFPB says your debt-to-income ratio (DTI) is all your monthly debt payments divided by your gross monthly income. Lenders use it as one way to measure your ability to manage the monthly payments to repay money you plan to borrow. The CFPB page was last reviewed August 28, 2023, so confirm current details with the CFPB.

Different limits

Different loan products and different lenders have different DTI limits.

How to calculate it

  1. Add up all your monthly debt payments
  2. Divide that total by your gross monthly income, which is generally what you earn before taxes and other deductions

The CFPB's example

Say you pay $1,500 a month for your mortgage, $100 for an auto loan, and $400 for the rest of your debts. Your monthly debt payments are $2,000 ($1,500 + $100 + $400). If your gross monthly income is $6,000, your DTI is 33 percent, since $2,000 is 33% of $6,000.
