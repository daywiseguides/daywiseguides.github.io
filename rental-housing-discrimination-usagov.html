Rental Housing Discrimination: How to Complain (USAGov)

USAGov explains where to complain if you think you were treated unfairly while trying to rent a home. Its page was last updated November 13, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

What it is

Housing discrimination happens when a housing provider or lending institution gets in the way of someone renting a place because of their race or color, national origin, religion, sex, family status (such as having children), or disability.

The law

USAGov says the Fair Housing Act makes housing discrimination illegal and links to a list of the types of discrimination it covers.

Where to complain

If you think you experienced housing discrimination, USAGov says to submit a housing complaint with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD).
