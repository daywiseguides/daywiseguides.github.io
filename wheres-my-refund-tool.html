About the IRS Where's My Refund? Tool (IRS)

This page covers the IRS tracker itself. For the overall refund timeline, see our other IRS guide below.

When it starts working

  • Within 24 hours after you e-file a current-year return
  • 3 or 4 days after you e-file a prior-year return
  • 4 weeks after you file a paper return

What it shows

Three stages: Return Received, Refund Approved, and Refund Sent. You get a personalized refund date after the IRS processes the return and approves the refund. You enter your Social Security number or ITIN, your filing status, and the exact whole-dollar refund amount.

What it cannot show

Amended returns (Form 1040-X) are not in the tool. The IRS points to Where's My Amended Return? instead. For refunds on business returns, the IRS lists 800-829-4933.

Lost or stolen check

Generally you can file an online claim for a replacement check if more than 28 days have passed since the IRS mailed the refund.
