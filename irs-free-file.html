IRS Free File: How It Works

When we checked on November 26, 2026, the IRS page said IRS Free File has closed and to check back in January 2027. This page summarizes how the program works.

Two options

  • Guided tax software, for adjusted gross income (AGI) of $95,000 or less. You pick an IRS partner company and answer questions. Some partners include a free state return.
  • Fillable Forms, for any income level. You enter your own information in online forms. There is no state filing.

How to start

The IRS says to always begin at IRS.gov/freefile. You will not get Free File if you go straight to a partner's own commercial site. Each guided partner sets its own rules on income, age, and state. The IRS says it does not endorse any individual partner.

What "free" means

It means eligible people can prepare and e-file a federal return at no cost. A guided partner may still charge for a state return, or if you do not qualify for its free federal offer. The IRS says the program bars upselling and hidden fees.

Good to know

  • Free File is for the current-year return only, not past-due returns.
  • To sign electronically you need last year's AGI or a self-select PIN. First-time filers can create a new PIN or enter $0 as AGI.
  • An extension (Form 4868) gives you until Oct. 15 to file, but you still need to pay any balance by April 15.
  • You can file an amended return in the same guided software, but Fillable Forms cannot prepare one.
