How to Place or Lift a Credit Freeze (USAGov)

USAGov says a credit freeze restricts access to your credit report and can help protect you from fraud if you suspect someone stole your information. Its page was last updated November 13, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

What it does

While a freeze is on, creditors cannot access your credit report. That stops them from approving a new account in your name, whether it is fraudulent or legitimate. To open new accounts yourself, you have to lift the freeze permanently or temporarily.

How to do it

It is free. Contact each of the three major credit reporting agencies (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) online, by phone, or by mail.

How long it takes

  • Freeze online or by phone: within one business day
  • Freeze by mail: within three business days
  • Lift online or by phone: within one hour
  • Lift by mail: within three business days
