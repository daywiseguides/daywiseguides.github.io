Creating a my Social Security Account (SSA)

The Social Security Administration (SSA) says a personal my Social Security account lets you securely access and manage your Social Security information online.

Who can make one

You must be 18 or older and have a valid email address.

Three steps

  1. Choose a sign-in service: Login.gov or ID.me. The SSA says that as of June 7, 2025, older email logins no longer work, so people without an account need to create one with one of these.
  2. Sign in to that service. If it is your first time, you verify your identity and create an account. The SSA says this one-time task can take 5 to 20 minutes.
  3. The SSA then signs you in to your my Social Security account.

Which to pick

The SSA says Login.gov is a government sign-in service used across many federal agencies and is best for most people in the United States. ID.me suits people inside or outside the U.S. For Login.gov you need an email address, a government-issued photo ID, and a phone number. For ID.me you need an email, a photo ID, and a device with a camera. If unsure, the SSA says to continue with Login.gov. Only start from ssa.gov.
