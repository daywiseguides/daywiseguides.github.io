How to Register to Vote (USAGov)

USAGov explains how to register so you can vote in federal, state, and local elections. Its page was last updated October 6, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Start at vote.gov

Visit vote.gov and select your state or territory. Depending on its rules, you will find instructions to register online, by mail, or in person at your local election office.

Other ways to register

  • Download the National Mail Voter Registration Form, fill it out onscreen or by hand, sign it, and mail it to the location listed for your state
  • Check with local government offices about registering in person
  • Go to your department of motor vehicles office
  • Contact state and county public assistance offices, such as SNAP or TANF offices

Abroad and military voters

The Federal Voting Assistance Program lets you register and request an absentee ballot if you are a U.S. citizen living outside the U.S., a service member stationed abroad, or the spouse or eligible family member of one.

Documents

Requirements vary by state. In most cases you need a driver's license or state ID. Without one, you may be able to use other documentation such as a bank statement or utility bill. Check with your state's election office.
