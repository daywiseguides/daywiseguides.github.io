SNAP (Food Stamps): How to Apply and Check Your Balance (USAGov)

USAGov says the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), formerly known as food stamps, can help you pay for food if you have a low income. Its page was last updated December 23, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

How it works

Each month, benefits are added to an Electronic Benefits Transfer (EBT) card that you use when you shop for food.

Eligibility

You must meet your state's requirements, which include income limits and the money you have in the bank.

How to apply

Contact your state or local SNAP office. Depending on your state, you may be able to apply online, in person, by mail, or by fax. You may need an interview before approval.

Check your balance

  • Look at the receipt from a store purchase, since many stores print the balance.
  • See whether your state offers a mobile app to manage benefits.
  • Contact your SNAP office.
