Complaint Against a Landlord: Where to Get Help (USAGov)

USAGov explains how to get help if you cannot settle a disagreement directly with your landlord or management company. Its page was last updated April 15, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Before you ask for outside help

USAGov says to read your lease carefully and make sure you are following the rental rules you agreed to.

Where to turn

  • Your state: find the state agency that handles tenant rights. Depending on the state, that may lead to the attorney general, a housing agency, or a state tenant rights handbook.
  • HUD: if the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development insures or manages your building, you can report the landlord's negligence or fraud to HUD's Multifamily Housing Complaint Line.
  • A lawyer: if you decide you need one, USAGov points to a guide on finding affordable legal aid.

Rules differ by state, so this page is a starting point and not legal advice.
