"Free Money" From the Government Is Usually a Scam (USAGov)

USAGov says promises of "grants" or "free money" from the government are often scams. They can arrive by text, email, ads, and websites. Its page was last updated September 23, 2026.

If you run into one

  • Look up the common signs of "free money" scams on USAGov
  • Report the scam to the Federal Trade Commission

Where real help is listed

USAGov points to free, official places to search for programs you might qualify for:

  • USAGov's benefit finder, for programs for you and your family
  • Its page on government grants and loans for living expenses and business costs
  • Grants.gov, for funding opportunities aimed at organizations and their projects

USAGov adds that each program has its own eligibility rules, application process, and deadlines.
