Retirement Planning Tools From the Government (USAGov)

USAGov says a retirement plan is a guide to reach your retirement goals, and lists tools that can help you build one. Its page was last updated July 30, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Savings worksheets

The Department of Labor offers interactive worksheets to help you set saving goals and timelines, decide how much to save each year, and organize your financial documents.

Social Security timing

You can start receiving Social Security retirement benefits as early as age 62. The later you apply, the higher your payments, but the amount stops increasing after age 70. A my Social Security account gives you an estimate of future benefits, and benefit calculators can show your full retirement age and spouse estimates.

Other government benefits

USAGov's benefit finder can point to retirement benefits for living expenses, health care, and medications. Federal employees can visit the OPM Retirement Center.

Your own accounts and cost of living

Your 401(k) or IRA provider may offer tools to track your progress. If you might move in retirement, online cost-of-living calculators let you compare places.
