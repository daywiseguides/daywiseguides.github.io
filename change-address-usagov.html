Changing Your Address: USPS and Government Services (USAGov)

USAGov explains how to change your address with the U.S. Postal Service and other government services so you keep getting mail after a move. Its page was last updated April 15, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

With the Postal Service

Tell the post office you are moving and want your mail forwarded. You can go to USPS.com/move to change your address online, or ask your local post office for the Mover's Guide packet.

A warning about fees

You do not need to pay a separate company. USAGov says scammers may charge $40 or more to do what you can do for $1.10 in the "Who is moving?" section of the official USPS.com website.
