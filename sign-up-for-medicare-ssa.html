Signing Up for Medicare Through Social Security (SSA)

The Social Security Administration (SSA) says Medicare is the national health insurance program for people age 65 or older, and that you sign up for Parts A and B through Social Security. We checked its page on November 26, 2026.

Who is covered

The age 65 group includes U.S. citizens or nationals, lawful permanent residents, Cuban-Haitian entrants, and Compacts of Free Association migrants. Under 65, you may qualify with permanent kidney failure or if you receive Disability benefits. SSA says Supplemental Security Income alone does not qualify you.

What to have ready

  • Your Social Security number and where you were born
  • Start and end dates for current group health plans, and for any after age 65
  • For Part B only: a valid email address and your existing Medicare number

How to apply

At 65 or older you can enroll online for Parts A and B, or Part A only. You can delay Part B if you are covered by an employer group health plan. If you have Part A and skipped Part B, you can sign up for Part B only. Medicare Advantage and drug plans are reviewed and chosen on Medicare.gov.

By phone, SSA lists 800-772-1213 (weekdays 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. in most U.S. time zones); say "Apply for Medicare". For kidney failure, call the same number and say that. After you apply, SSA's page links to an application status check.
