How to Check Your IRS Refund Status

The IRS refunds page says status is available 24 hours after you e-file a current-year return, 3 days after you e-file a prior-year return, and 4 weeks after you file a paper return.

Ways to check

  • Sign in to your IRS online account to see refund status, tax records, and amended return status.
  • Use the refund tracker without signing in. You need your exact refund amount, your Social Security number or ITIN, your filing status, and the tax year.
  • Use the IRS app.
  • Call the automated hotline: 800-829-1954 for refunds, 866-464-2050 for amended returns.

When to expect it

The IRS gives a typical time of 3 weeks from the date you e-filed, or 6 or more weeks from the date it received a mailed return. Delays can happen when a return needs corrections or further review.

Only enter your information on the official IRS site at irs.gov. Never share it with someone who contacts you out of the blue.
