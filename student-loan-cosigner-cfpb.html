Co-Signing a Student Loan: Risks for Borrower and Co-Signer (CFPB)

The CFPB says a co-signer is a person who agrees to repay a loan along with the primary borrower. Its page was last reviewed September 6, 2024, so confirm current details with the CFPB.

For the borrower

You may need a co-signer to qualify for a private student loan, or want one to get a lower interest rate thanks to their credit record. Before asking someone, read the loan documents carefully. You must make every payment on time, because late or missed payments affect both your credit history and the co-signer's.

For the co-signer

  • Read the loan documents carefully
  • You are equally responsible and legally obligated to repay. Ask whether you are willing and able to pay if the student does not
  • Late or missed payments affect your credit record too
  • If the loan goes into default, private lenders often hire collection agencies to get a co-signer to pay, and you could be sued by a debt collector or lender
