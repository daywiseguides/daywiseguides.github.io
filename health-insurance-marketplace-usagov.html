Health Insurance Marketplace: Eligibility and How to Enroll (USAGov)

USAGov says the Affordable Care Act (ACA) Health Insurance Marketplace helps people find more affordable health insurance. Its page was last updated November 21, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026, so check Healthcare.gov for current rules and dates.

Who can use it

As of that page, there is no income limit. To enroll you must live in the United States, be a U.S. citizen or national or be lawfully present, and not be incarcerated.

Protections it lists

  • Insurers cannot refuse coverage based on sex or a pre-existing condition
  • No lifetime or annual limits on coverage for essential health benefits
  • Young adults can stay on a family plan until age 26

What it costs

What you pay may depend on where you live, your income, and your household size. Costs include a monthly premium, plus out-of-pocket costs such as deductibles, coinsurance, and copayments if you use care.

How to enroll

Plans cover medical care and also dental and vision. Start at Healthcare.gov to find your state's Marketplace, since each has its own enrollment steps. During the yearly open enrollment period you can choose a plan, keep your plan, change it, or replace it. A life event like moving or having a baby may allow a special enrollment period, and a low household income may also qualify.
