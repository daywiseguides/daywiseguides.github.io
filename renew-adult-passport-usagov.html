Renewing an Adult U.S. Passport: Who Can, and How (USAGov)

USAGov says to renew early, since processing times vary and some countries and airlines deny entry if your passport expires in under 6 months. Its page was last updated March 23, 2026.

You cannot renew if your passport was

  • Issued before your 16th birthday
  • Issued more than 15 years ago
  • Damaged, lost, or stolen
  • Issued in a previous name, with no legal document such as a marriage license proving the change

In those cases you apply for a new passport in person with Form DS-11. An expired passport can be renewed only if it was issued within the last 15 years.

By mail

You typically need Form DS-82, a passport photo, the fee, your most recent passport, and name-change papers if needed. The old passport comes back separately, usually about 4 weeks after the new one.

Online

Some people can renew online. USAGov says the State Department runs the only authorized place to do it.

In a hurry, or outside the U.S.

Expedited and urgent options carry extra fees and vary by season. Abroad, contact the nearest U.S. embassy or consulate. You can check status online or by phone.
