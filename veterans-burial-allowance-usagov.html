Veterans Burial Allowance: Who Qualifies (USAGov)

USAGov explains that a veterans burial allowance helps pay for transportation, funeral, and burial costs for an eligible veteran. If you qualify, the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) repays a fixed amount. USAGov's page was last updated August 27, 2025, so confirm current amounts with the VA.

The veteran must

  • Not have been dishonorably discharged
  • Also meet one more condition: receiving care from a VA facility, receiving a VA pension, or dying from a service-connected disability

You must

  • Have paid for the burial, transportation, or funeral costs
  • Be a close relative of the veteran or have managed their estate

Applying

A surviving spouse does not have to file a claim for burial costs. USAGov says the VA automatically pays a set amount after it receives notice of the veteran's death. Others can apply online through the VA's process. The VA's pages explain the amounts, which depend on when the veteran died and whether the death was related to military service, and how long after the burial you have to file.

Planning burial in a national cemetery is a separate step with its own eligibility rules.
