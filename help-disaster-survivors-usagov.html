Helping Disaster Survivors: Donate, Volunteer, Find Loved Ones (USAGov)

USAGov points to FEMA's guidance for helping after a disaster. Its page was last updated March 23, 2026.

Donating

  • Money is the best gift: it lets responders buy supplies in bulk. Give to a trusted organization working at the disaster site.
  • Items: give only what is requested, only items on the published lists, and do not send items directly to the affected area. Use a relief organization or the government.
  • Blood is always needed, especially after a disaster.

Questions about donations can go to FEMA at donations@fema.dhs.gov.

Volunteering

Register with an organization that was recruited to help, and do not go on your own. Wait until it tells you whether it wants to deploy you. USAGov links a directory of disaster relief groups and the Red Cross volunteer search.

Finding loved ones

DisasterAssistance.gov has information on finding family, unaccompanied children, and missing pets. The Red Cross also offers tips for contacting people in affected areas.
