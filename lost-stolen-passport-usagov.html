Lost or Stolen Passport: What to Do (USAGov)

USAGov says if your passport is lost or stolen you must report it to the State Department right away. Its page was last updated March 24, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Report it

  • Online: submit Form DS-64 with the online tool
  • By phone: 1-877-487-2778 (TTY 1-888-874-7793)
  • By mail: send Form DS-64 to the address on the form

Once reported, the State Department invalidates the passport. You cannot use it if you find it later.

Replace it

In the U.S.: you must apply in person for a new passport using Form DS-11. USAGov says to follow State Department guidance based on how fast you need it, and to check current processing times.

Outside the U.S.: contact your nearest U.S. embassy or consulate. In some cases you may get a limited-validity passport if there is not enough time for a regular one.

If you find someone else's passport

A U.S. passport found in the U.S. should be mailed to the State Department's Consular Lost and Stolen Passport Unit (the address is on USAGov's page). Found abroad, return it to the nearest U.S. embassy or consulate. A foreign passport found in the U.S. goes to that country's embassy or consulate.
