Child Tax Credit and Credit for Other Dependents (USAGov)

USAGov says if you are responsible for a child or other dependent, you may qualify for a credit on your tax return. Its page was last updated December 22, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Child Tax Credit

USAGov says you may qualify even if you do not normally file a tax return. Eligibility depends on your income, the child's age, how you are related to the child, and other factors. USAGov links to the IRS pages on how the credit works, who qualifies, and how to claim it.

Credit for Other Dependents

If you are not eligible for the Child Tax Credit, you may be able to claim the Credit for Other Dependents. USAGov gives the maximum as $500 for each dependent. Eligibility depends on your income and whether your dependent qualifies.

Check your eligibility

USAGov points to an IRS tool that helps you find out whether you qualify for either credit. This page is a summary, so use the IRS pages for current amounts and rules.
