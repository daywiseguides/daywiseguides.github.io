Dispute Errors on Your Credit Report (USAGov)

USAGov says errors on your credit report can affect your credit score and your ability to get a loan or rent a property. Its page was last updated August 31, 2026.

What errors look like

Examples USAGov gives include the wrong name or address on an account, or an incorrect date you made a payment. If you see accounts you did not open, that may be identity theft, and USAGov links to how to report it.

How to fix one

  • Gather documents that support your case.
  • Write a letter disputing the error and include the documents.
  • Send it to the credit reporting agency whose report you are disputing, and to the company that supplied the wrong information.

If the dispute does not work

USAGov says you can file a complaint with the CFPB if your written dispute with the credit bureau does not fix the error.
