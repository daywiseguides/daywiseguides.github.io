Emergency Rent Assistance: Where to Start (USAGov)

USAGov says to contact your state to find emergency help paying your rent. Its page was last updated April 15, 2026 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Where to look

Each state has its own eligibility rules. To find programs near you, USAGov says to call 211 or search for state and local emergency rental assistance programs.

If you do not qualify

Your state or local agency may refer you to a community or nonprofit organization that can help.

Counseling and legal help

  • To talk to a housing counselor, find your local Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) participating counseling agency.
  • If you have a disability and need legal help, contact the National Disability Rights Network in your state or territory.
