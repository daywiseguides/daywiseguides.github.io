Unemployment Benefits: Who Qualifies and How to Apply (USAGov)

USAGov says unemployment insurance pays you money if you lose your job through no fault of your own. Its page was last updated March 28, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

No federal program

There is no federal unemployment program. Each state runs its own and pays the benefits, so the rules depend on your state. USAGov provides a state map to find eligibility rules and filing instructions, and you may be able to file online or by phone.

Common eligibility rules

Many states require that you earned at least a certain amount in the last 12 to 24 months, worked consistently over that time, and look for a new job.

Where to file

In most cases, file in the state where you worked, not the state where you live. If you worked in another state or remotely, USAGov says to contact your home state's unemployment office for help filing a claim in a different state.

Related help

  • Health coverage: the law known as COBRA lets qualified workers keep group health insurance for a limited time after a change in eligibility.
  • Scams: scammers file claims using other people's names and information. USAGov has a separate page on protecting yourself.
