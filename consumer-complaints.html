Where to File a Consumer Complaint (USAGov)

USAGov has a hub of guides on filing complaints about products and services. Its page lists these topics.

Topics covered

  • Online purchases: how to resolve it with the seller or website, or get help from a federal or state agency.
  • A company's products or services: file with the company first, then seek consumer or legal help if it is not resolved.
  • Your car: where to go if the dealer, auto loan company, or manufacturer does not fix the issue.
  • Phone and text scams, robocalls, and telemarketers, including the Do Not Call Registry.
  • Phone company complaints.
  • Cable or satellite TV complaints.

Need help finding the right one?

USAGov lists a phone number on the page: 1-844-USAGOV1 (1-844-872-4681).
