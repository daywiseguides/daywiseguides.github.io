Using BLS Data in Your Job Search

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) says its data can help you explore occupations, see job outlook, and find wage data for where you live or work.

Explore careers

The Occupational Outlook Handbook has quick links to topics like the highest paying occupations and the fastest projected growth. You can also combine pay range, education and training needs, and projections to build a list of occupations.

Check wages near you

The Occupational Employment and Wages Statistics program gives employment and wage estimates by occupation at the national, state, and metro and non-metro area levels. You can use them to find top-paying industries and areas, or to prepare for a salary talk.

Learn what a job requires

The Occupational Requirements Survey covers physical demands, work conditions, education, training and experience, and cognitive and mental requirements.

More help

BLS points to the Department of Labor's CareerOneStop job search tools for resumes, networking, interviewing, salary negotiation, and finding openings.
