Replacing Lost or Stolen ID Cards: Who to Contact (USAGov)

USAGov says rules for replacing government-issued ID cards vary, and many require an official document such as a birth certificate to prove identity or citizenship. Its page was last updated November 17, 2025 when we checked on November 26, 2026.

Who to contact, by document

  • Driver's license or state ID: your state motor vehicle agency
  • Social Security card: the Social Security Administration, online, by mail, or in person
  • Birth certificate: the vital records office in your birth state or territory, for a certified copy
  • Medicare card: log in or create a Medicare account online to print a copy or request a mailed replacement, or call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227, TTY 1-877-486-2048)
  • Medicaid card: your state Medicaid office
  • U.S. passport: report it lost or stolen to the State Department, then request a replacement in person
  • Permanent Resident (Green) Card: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
  • Naturalization certificate: Form N-565 to replace a Certificate of Citizenship
  • Military ID card: report it to your unit security officer, then make an appointment at an ID Card office
