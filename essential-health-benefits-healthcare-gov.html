The 10 Essential Health Benefits Every Marketplace Plan Covers (HealthCare.gov)

HealthCare.gov says every plan offered in the Marketplace covers 10 essential health benefits. The page shows no update date, so we checked it on November 26, 2026.

The 10

  1. Ambulatory patient services (outpatient care without a hospital admission)
  2. Emergency services
  3. Hospitalization, such as surgery and overnight stays
  4. Pregnancy, maternity, and newborn care
  5. Mental health and substance use disorder services, including counseling and psychotherapy
  6. Prescription drugs
  7. Rehabilitative and habilitative services and devices
  8. Laboratory services
  9. Preventive and wellness services and chronic disease management
  10. Pediatric services, including oral and vision care (adult dental and vision are not essential health benefits)

Also required, and extras

Plans must include birth control and breastfeeding coverage. Some offer more, such as dental, vision, or programs for weight management, back pain, and diabetes. All Marketplace plans include children's vision coverage; only some include adult vision.

Good to know

  • This applies to every metal level, including Catastrophic, and every plan type such as HMO and PPO
  • Benefits are generally the same across states, but some states require more, and small differences exist even within a state
  • You still generally pay deductibles and copayments for these benefits, though preventive services are free
  • To check a specific treatment, compare plans in the Marketplace or call the plan
  • Large self-insured employers do not have to provide essential health benefits, though many do; ask your employer
